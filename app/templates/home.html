Inventory Control Platform

Warehouse Inventory Management System

A warehouse inventory system built with Flask and PostgreSQL to manage items, suppliers, stock movement, purchase orders, reporting, and role-based access control in one structured application.

Create Initial Data Login to System

Initial data setup will create warehouse records, suppliers, items, purchase orders, transactions, and role-based user accounts for demonstration and testing.

Inventory Tracking

Monitor stock quantities, reorder levels, storage locations, and item details across the warehouse.

Supplier Management

Maintain supplier records and link suppliers to items through a many-to-many relationship with supply prices.

Stock Transactions

Record stock coming in and going out while preserving transaction history for accountability and auditing.

Purchase Orders & Reports

Create purchase orders, manage procurement activity, and view reports for stock levels, value, and purchase history.

System Modules

The application demonstrates the main database-backed operations expected in a warehouse environment.

Module Purpose
Items Create, update, track, and monitor inventory records.
Suppliers Maintain supplier details and item-supplier relationships.
Transactions Record stock receipts and dispatches while preserving history.
Purchase Orders Create supplier orders and track ordered line items.
Reports View low-stock alerts, stock valuation, and purchase order history.
Database Notes Summarize the relational design, normalization, and key implementation ideas.
